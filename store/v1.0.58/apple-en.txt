When you ask the agent to restart, shut down or log out of your desktop, it now finishes its answer first, then counts down on a card you can stop.

The card appears in the chat with the action's name, a bar draining over a few seconds, and an Abort button that works from your phone. Let the bar empty and the action runs. Press Abort and it never does.

Open the conversation later and the card tells you what happened: it ran, you aborted it, or it was dropped because the turn was stopped before its answer arrived.
